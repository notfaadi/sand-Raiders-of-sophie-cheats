## Production checklist (per post)

1. Confirm Status / Features copy still matches the product (other teams may edit brand).  
2. Title via SEO formula spirit: `{Topic} | Sand Raiders` when used in `<title>` helpers; on-page H1 can stay short.  
3. Meta ~140–160 chars: benefit + what’s included + Windows PC.  
4. Internal links: Features, Status, Store, Setup, Reviews — not orphan posts.  
5. One primary CTA; one secondary link.  
6. Add source entry in `scripts/generate-blog-posts.mjs` → run generator → verify `/blog/{slug}/`.  
7. After real EFT patches, bump `updated` date and add a short Status callout — do not rewrite the whole calendar.
